## 2021
{: .archive__subtitle }

### Who's In Control? On Security Risks of Disjointed IoT Device Management Channels
{: #iot-management-channels .archive__item-title }

Y. Jia, B. Yan, L. Xing, D. Zhao, X. Wang, **Y. Zhang**, Y. Liu, K. Zheng, Y. Zhang, D. Zou, H. Jin
{: .small }

**ACM CCS 2021** · [PDF]({{ site.paper_baseurl | append: '/CCS21_DMC.pdf' | relative_url }})

<details>
<summary>Research impact</summary>
<p>We disclosed vulnerabilities in widely used IoT devices and systems that could let attackers control other users' devices and steal personal data. Affected manufacturers included iRobot, August, Yale, Philips Hue, Tuya, Amazon Alexa, Belkin, and Dyson. Vendors acknowledged the issues and adopted our security designs and patches to protect their products.</p>
</details>
